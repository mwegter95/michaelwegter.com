Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

This policy covers Life Dashboard and the other personal apps published at michaelwegter.com (the “Apps”). They are built and operated by Michael Wegter as independent personal projects, not by or on behalf of any employer or client.

The short version: the Apps collect only what they need in order to work, the data lives on a server I run, nothing is sold or used for advertising, and you can delete all of it at any time.

1. Who is responsible

Michael Wegter, Minnesota, United States. Contact: mwegter95@gmail.com. For privacy questions, please put “Privacy” in the subject line.

2. What the Apps collect

Account information

If you create an account you provide an email address, a password, and optionally a display name. Passwords are stored only as a salted hash — they are never stored or transmitted in readable form. You can also use Life Dashboard without an account, in which case the App generates a random device token stored in your browser and your data is tied to that token rather than to you personally.

Content you create

Habits and to-dos, the days you check them off and the points earned, end-of-day reflections, and your mantra. This is the substance of the App and is visible only to you.

Google Calendar data (optional)

Connecting Google Calendar is entirely optional; every other feature works without it. If you connect it, the App requests a single read-only scope, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly, and uses it to read your upcoming events for roughly the next four months so it can suggest dated reminders — a gift before a birthday, packing before a trip, paperwork before an appointment.

ItemHow it is handled
OAuth refresh tokenStored encrypted at rest on the server. Never shown, logged or shared.
Your Google account emailStored so the App can show you which calendar is connected.
Event title, date, location, calendarFetched live when you open the dashboard or when reminders are generated. Not stored as a copy — only the short reminder text the App generates is saved.
Everything else in your calendarNot requested. The scope is read-only, so the App cannot create, edit or delete anything in your calendar.

What is not collected

No third-party analytics, no advertising or tracking pixels, no cookies beyond what is needed to keep you signed in, no location data, no contacts, no payment information. The Apps are free and there is nothing to buy.

3. How the data is used

  • To show you your own habits, streaks, points and reminders.
  • To keep you signed in across sessions and devices.
  • To turn upcoming calendar events into suggested reminders, if you connected a calendar.
  • To keep the service running — diagnosing errors and preventing abuse.

Your data is not used to train any machine-learning model, is not sold or rented, is not used for advertising or profiling, and is not shared with data brokers.

4. Google user data and the Limited Use requirements

Limited Use disclosure

Life Dashboard’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Concretely, that means Google Calendar data is:

  • used only to provide and improve the reminder features you can see in the App;
  • never transferred to anyone else except as needed to provide those features, to comply with applicable law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with prior notice;
  • never used for advertising of any kind;
  • never sold; and
  • never read by a human, except where you give explicit consent for a specific support issue, where it is required by law, or where it is necessary for security purposes such as investigating abuse.

6. Where the data lives, and for how long

App data is stored in a database on a private server I operate, reachable at api.michaelwegter.com, in the United States. Traffic to it is encrypted in transit (HTTPS), and the Google refresh token is additionally encrypted at rest.

Data is kept until you delete it. Anonymous device-token data that has gone unused for a long period may be removed during routine maintenance.

7. Your choices and how to delete your data

  • Disconnect Google Calendar. Use the Disconnect button in the dashboard’s Calendar panel. The stored refresh token is deleted from the server immediately.
  • Revoke access from Google. You can remove the App at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete individual content. Habits, reminders and reflections can be removed from within the App.
  • Delete everything. Email mwegter95@gmail.com from your account’s address and I will delete the account and all associated data, normally within 30 days.

Depending on where you live you may also have the right to access, correct, export or restrict the processing of your personal data, and to complain to a supervisory authority. Email me and I will help.

8. Security

Passwords are hashed, the OAuth refresh token is encrypted at rest, and all traffic runs over HTTPS. That said, this is a personal project maintained by one person — no system is perfectly secure, and you should not store anything in it that you would be harmed by losing or disclosing.

9. Children

The Apps are not directed at children under 13 and I do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information, email me and I will delete it.

10. Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top will change and, where the change affects data already collected, the App will say so. Continuing to use the Apps after a change means you accept the updated policy.
